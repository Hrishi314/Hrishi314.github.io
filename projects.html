EURION PATTERN DETECTOR

Course Project · Basics of Image Processing (EP19KM17)
Guide: Prof. Vincent Mazet, University of Strasbourg

  • Engineered a fully automated image processing pipeline to detect EURion constellation security patterns across diverse currency designs and image conditions.
